Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

STARVISTA ("we", "our", "us") is a property management application designed to help property owners and authorized users manage properties, rooms and resident information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

By using STARVISTA, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use STARVISTA, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication information required to create and access an account.
  • Property Information: Property name, address, room information and property images that you choose to upload.
  • Resident Information: Name, gender, email address, phone number, room number, rent amount, start/end dates and optional remarks entered by an authorized property manager.
  • Application Information: Basic information needed to keep the application working, authenticate users and maintain app sessions.
  • Device Permissions: The application may request access to photos or storage when you choose to select an image for a property.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and authenticate user accounts.
  • To provide property, room and resident management features.
  • To store and display information entered by authorized users.
  • To upload and display property images selected by users.
  • To maintain application security, reliability and functionality.
  • To respond to support requests and resolve technical issues.

3. Third-Party Services

STARVISTA uses third-party infrastructure and services to provide its features, including Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage, and image-hosting infrastructure for property images where applicable.

These providers may process information on our behalf according to their respective privacy practices.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate STARVISTA, or disclosed when required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the service.

6. Resident Information and Authorized Use

STARVISTA allows property owners or authorized property managers to enter resident information.

Users are responsible for having an appropriate legal basis and permission to collect and manage information they enter about other individuals.

Users should not enter information that they are not authorized to collect or manage.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and application data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

When data is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of, subject to applicable requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting us using the support contact below.

We may need to verify the request before processing it.

Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and record-keeping purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

STARVISTA is intended for property-management use and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
